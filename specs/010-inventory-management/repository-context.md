## Confirmed answers and declared defaults

The initial three clarification markers are now resolved. Q1: initial positive stock and replenishment are future admin functions; zero initialization/test-only explicit balances do not create stock administration. Q2: customer confirmation remains future work. Q3:A: full resulting quantity checks on add/update/switch/true selection; eligible select-all/counts/totals and no silent quantity/preference rewrites. The follow-up explicitly approves FAILED for future receipt refusal, but defers SHIPPING-to-FAILED and all failed-order processing. No payment flow, expiry policy or new public terminal action is invented.

The brief's recommended missing-record/full-row-skip policies are explicit defaults; legacy exemption/no backfill and existing normal-gate concurrency practice are retained. Future genuine terminal failure/expiry releases once, retryable payment failure retains allocations and payment success alone never deducts physical stock. These future workflow requirements do not turn absent producers into implemented capabilities. All business clarifications for this specify scope are resolved.

Existing active legacy orders do not automatically receive reservations. New managed orders must be distinguishable even when their reservation is corrupt/missing. A later approved backfill is separate; an aggregate reserved balance is never proof that a legacy order owns stock. Active reservation deletion safety must be reconciled with historical catalog-reference deletion in planning.
